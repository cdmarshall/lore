The ledger

The Quest Log

Your one list of action items. You work in it; Lore works in it too, and changes appear for both of you as they happen.

The triage pile

Items Lore finds in meetings and messages arrive as cards, one at a time, oldest first. One click decides each:

ButtonResult
Do nowActive, due ASAP, top of the list.
Already doneStraight to Completed, today's date.
Schedule…Active with the date or words you pick.
Delegate…Straight to Delegated: a teammate, or any name you type.
DeleteGone from view, and Lore remembers you threw it away so it never proposes it again. Undo covers a mis-click.
Working the list

Check it off: the row strikes through for five seconds, then completes. Uncheck to undo. Edit in place: click a subject, source, due date or note. Filters: Overdue, Due Soon, ASAP, TBD, Lore-Ready, Specialist-Ready, Stale. Flags: Lore means Lore could do it for you; Specialist means a companion agent could.

Backups and sharing

Download snapshot saves a markdown copy; Restore from backup puts one back after asking. The tracker is private to you. Share it from its Share menu and editors can work the list too.

Connecting something new

Add the connector in claude.ai under Settings, then Connectors (or the connectors menu in a Cowork chat, or /mcp in Claude Code), then tell Lore: "I connected Linear" or "check my connectors." Lore confirms it works with one read-only call, asks the few scoping questions it needs (your channels, your project keys), and adds it to your profile. If it unlocks the daily brief, Lore offers to schedule it.

Changing tools

Say "I switched email to Gmail" and Lore updates that one role. A tool with no connector yet is recorded honestly: Lore tells you it can't read it, and you forward or paste what you want it to see.

What Lore will never do

Use a connector you didn't name for that job, write anything while testing a connector, or look outside the channels, projects and spaces you gave it.

Inside the Notion library

A private Lore page in your Notion space holds five databases: People, Projects, Meetings (each linking back to its recording), Decisions and Notes, plus a Context page and a Commitments page.

In ObsidianIn Notion

Moving house

Say "migrate to Notion" and Lore counts what you have, shows three pages as they'll look, and waits for your yes. It copies people first, then everything that links to them, and leaves the originals where they were until you remove them.

Who can see it

Pages in your private Notion space are yours. On some Notion plans, workspace admins can still reach private content; keep that in mind for 1:1 notes.

Getting the most from recaps

Recaps count what happened in your Claude sessions as well as your messages and notes. Cowork sessions are read directly. For Claude Code sessions, run node scripts/build-cc-session-digest.js --days 90 from Claude Code in your Lore folder now and then; Lore tells you when its view is out of date and always says which sessions a recap covered.

Teaching Lore

Correct anything it gets wrong, in plain words. Lore fixes the output and writes the lesson into the file that prevents a repeat, then tells you where. Style corrections become rules in VOICE.md; facts go to the right page.

VOICE.md also lists the habits that make writing read as machine-made, and Lore checks every draft against it. A hook flags the clear-cut ones (curly quotes, stock AI words, flattering openers) the moment a file is written. Words your context.md glossary or a source document defines are never treated as jargon. Signed documents now end on the signet and "Lore" alone, with no dash in front.

Remedies

When something looks off

Most surprises have a one-line fix.

You seeTry
The Quest Log says it can't reach its databaseOpen it on claude.ai while signed in, or reload it. It saves nothing until it reconnects.
Lore says it can't find your Quest LogSay "find my quest log". If you never moved to the new tracker, say "run migrations".
The Herald skipped your email or chat"Check my connectors". A connector that needs signing in again shows as needs reconnecting.
A source shows as "not swept yet"Lore knows you use that tool but can't read it yet. Forward or paste what matters.
Times in a brief are an hour off"My timezone is [zone]". Use the name, like America/Phoenix, not a region.
Lore can't reach your notesThe folder isn't connected to this session, or Notion is disconnected. Add it back in the project settings; Lore won't write notes anywhere else meanwhile.
A draft replies to something you already answeredTell Lore. It checks the thread's last message before every draft and treats a miss as a lesson to fix.
An update was offered but you're busySay "later". Nothing changes until you say "update".
Keeping this book current

Every Lore release that changes what you see or say updates this grimoire, starting with What's new. The newest copy is always at docs/lore-grimoire.pdf in your Lore folder.
